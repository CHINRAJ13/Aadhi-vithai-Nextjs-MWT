# Aadhi Vithai Design System: Sacred Light

## Creative North Star
**"The Sacred Light"**
The aesthetic is vast, clear, and pure. It avoids the typical temple look, opting for a "Modern Spiritual" luxury feel. It feels like an open, sun-drenched sanctuary—spacious, profound, and premium.

## Colors
- **Pure White (#FFFFFF):** The foundation of infinite space and clarity.
- **Soft Pearl (#FAFAFA):** For subtle depth and sectioning.
- **Muted Gold (#D4AF37):** Representing the inner flame and sacredness.
- **Deep Charcoal (#1A1A1A):** For high-readability text that feels authoritative and premium.

## Typography
- **Headlines (Newsreader/Serif):** Elegant, heritage-driven, and authoritative.
- **Body (Inter/Sans-Serif):** Modern, clear, and highly readable.

## Textures & Effects
- **White Glassmorphism:** Semi-transparent white cards with backdrop-blur for a layered, ethereal feel.
- **Ambient Shadows:** Very soft, diffused "natural light" shadows to create lift and depth.
- **Whitespace:** Used as a core design element to emphasize stillness and clarity.

## Shapes
- **Rounded Corners:** 12px - 16px to feel welcoming and soft.
